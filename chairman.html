Teachers' Day · A Note of Gratitude

A Letter of Gratitude

To the person who taught us that success is much bigger than business.
✦ ✦ ✦

Dear Sir,

There are some people whose success can be measured by the size of the organisation they have built.

And then there are people whose success is measured by the number of lives they have influenced along the way.

You belong to the second kind.

When we look at the journey of The House of Shubhashish, we don't just see a company that has grown. We see a vision that started somewhere, took shape through countless decisions, challenges and years of hard work, and today stands where it does because someone believed that it could.

But what inspires us the most is not simply how much you have built.

It is the person you have remained while building it.

From you, we have learned that being successful is not only about having a successful business.

It is about becoming a successful human being.

"Success is not only about what we achieve,
but also about who we become while achieving it."

You have shown us that a person's real worth isn't defined by the money they possess, the position they hold, or the power that comes with their designation.

It is defined by the respect they give to others.

And perhaps that is one of the most beautiful lessons we have learned from watching you.

❦

You have made us understand that success doesn't have to make a person distant. It can make a person more humble.

It doesn't have to make someone feel superior. It can make them more respectful.

And it doesn't have to be about asking, "How much have I achieved?"

Sometimes, it is about asking, "How many people became better because they met me?"

A Lesson Beyond Business

What We Have Learned From You

✦ ✦ ✦

Sir, your journey has taught us something that no business book can completely explain.

"A successful person is not simply someone who reaches the top. A successful person is someone who remembers to look around after reaching there."

You have made us realise that the way we speak to someone, the way we treat someone, and the respect we offer to someone — these things matter.

A lot.

Because positions change.

Designations change.

Businesses grow.

Numbers go up and down.

But the way we make people feel stays with them.

And perhaps that is why your journey inspires us beyond the walls of business.

You have given us an example of what it means to build something meaningful while also building a reputation based on respect, relationships and values.

Greatness doesn't always need to announce itself.
Sometimes, greatness simply makes everyone else feel important.

So, on this Teachers' Day, we wanted to say something that perhaps doesn't get said enough:

Thank you, Sir.

Thank you for giving us more than an organisation to work for.

Thank you for giving us a vision to believe in.

Thank you for showing us what ambition can build.

And most importantly, thank you for showing us what kind of person we can become while pursuing success.

"Success is not just about how high you rise. It is about how many people you continue to respect on your way there."

And perhaps that is the legacy that lasts the longest.

Not the money.
Not the position.
Not even the buildings.

But the respect you leave behind in the hearts of people.

❦

With deepest respect and gratitude,

From all of us who have had the privilege
of learning from you.

Happy Teachers' Day, Sir.
